The Tournament

A Week That
Doesn't Repeat

A Cooperstown tournament is more than a baseball trip — it's a milestone. Our players will compete on the same fields where baseball's history was made, trade pins with teams from across the country, and spend a full week together at the home of the game.

For many of these players, it will be their first time competing on a national stage and staying with their team for a full week. It's an experience that shapes character as much as it does skill. You only get one summer at this age — this is the one.
